What We Offer

Healthcare professionals, placed where they are needed most

Vanguard Health Group is a healthcare labour hire agency. We supply qualified nursing and support professionals to aged care providers, disability organisations, hospitals, and community health services across Australia.

We manage screening, compliance, and workforce coordination end to end, so your organisation can focus on delivering quality care without the overhead of direct recruitment.

Nursing

Registered Nurses (RNs), Enrolled Nurses (ENs), and Clinical Specialist Nurses across acute care, aged care, disability services, and community health.

Aged Care & Disability Support

Support workers for personal care, residential aged care facilities, and NDIS and community settings.
